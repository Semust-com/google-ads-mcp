# Google Ads MCP Server: prompts to copy

Paste these into Claude, Claude Code or Cursor once the server is set up with `SEMUST_MCP_TOOLSETS=gads`. If you have several Semust projects, name the one you mean, or the assistant will ask. Dates are worked out for you; say "last 30 days" or "last month".

## Account overview

- How much did total cost and conversions change versus last month?
- Rank my campaigns by cost and show ROAS and cost per conversion for each.
- How do spend and ROAS split between my Performance Max and Search campaigns?

## Spend

- On which days did my daily spend spike in the last 30 days?
- What is my average daily spend, and which campaign takes the biggest share?
- Suggest a budget shift from low-ROAS campaigns to high-ROAS ones.

## Search terms

- Which search terms cost money in the last 30 days but brought no conversions? Suggest negative keyword candidates.
- Which search terms bring the most conversions, and at what cost each?

## Conversions

- Which conversion action brings the most value?
- How many conversions did each action record this month, and what were they worth?

## Devices and locations

- Is my cost per conversion higher on mobile than on desktop?
- Which countries or locations convert at the lowest cost?
- Which device brings the highest ROAS?

## Reporting

- Write a weekly Google Ads summary for my client: spend, conversions, ROAS and three things to try next.
- What is the ROAS of my 5 highest-spending campaigns this month?
